## 4. User-Generated Product Images (Lender Uploads)

In accordance with Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 (Safe Harbor) and Section 3 of the IT Rules 2021:
1. **Lender Warranty**: Under Section 4 of the [paYent Terms of Service](/terms), every lender listing equipment warrants that all uploaded photos are either original photographs captured by the lender or that they hold authorized commercial rights to upload said images.
2. **Platform Intermediary Role**: paYent functions purely as an intermediary facilitating peer-to-peer equipment rentals and does not initiate, select, or modify user-uploaded listing content.

## 5. Statutory Notice & Takedown Procedure (Indian Copyright Act, 1957)

If any copyright owner, photographer, or authorized agent believes that any media asset displayed on the paYent platform infringes upon their copyright under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, they may submit a formal **Notice of Infringement** to our designated Grievance & Copyright Officer.

### Required Notice Contents:
1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
2. Direct URL or screenshot of the infringing material on `payent.com` or `payent.in`.
3. Proof of ownership or authorization from the copyright holder.
4. Contact details of the complainant (full name, address, telephone number, email).
5. A signed declaration stating: *"I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."*

### Submission Channel:
- **Designated Officer**: Grievance & Copyright Redressal Officer
- **Email**: `grievance@payent.com`
- **Official Helpline**: `+91 79890 02612`
- **Statutory Turnaround**: Acknowledged within 48 hours; expeditious takedown or response within 15–30 days pursuant to IT Rules 2021.
